# Licensing and third-party materials

**Commercial-intent engineering repository.** A public GitHub repository is not automatically an open hardware license.

## Original AVTOSTAR material

At this preliminary stage no blanket permission to reproduce or commercially manufacture the original AVTOSTAR PCB/layout, enclosure or branding is granted. A hardware license and contributor agreement can be chosen before the first public manufacturing release.

## rusEFI firmware

- [rusEFI sources](https://github.com/rusefi/rusefi)
- [rusEFI license information](https://github.com/rusefi/rusefi/blob/master/LICENSE)
- Firmware redistributions must comply with the actual licenses of the specific source files and dependencies, including GPL source-delivery requirements where applicable.
- Board configuration and modifications linked into a GPL-covered firmware build may need to be distributed as corresponding source.
- Do not copy third-party protected trademarks or claim rusEFI endorsement.

## Hardware references

A schematic being publicly visible does **not** necessarily grant permission to make and sell derived hardware. Audit and record licenses **per source/revision** before copying circuitry, KiCad files, symbols, footprints or layouts. See [docs/references.md](docs/references.md).

## TunerStudio

TunerStudio is third-party software from EFI Analytics. Device compatibility does not grant permission to bundle, resell or sublicense the application. Confirm the commercial terms before shipping software or licenses with a device.

## Before first sale

- Record provenance and licenses of all code and CAD files.
- Determine whether hardware-source publication is required by any incorporated license.
- Deliver the appropriate notices and firmware source / installation information.
- Verify applicable technical and regulatory requirements for the intended markets and use cases.
