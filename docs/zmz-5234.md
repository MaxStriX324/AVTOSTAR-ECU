## Data to capture before wiring

Exact injector part numbers and impedance, fuel pressure/regulator, intake/fuel rail arrangement, CKP/CMP sensor types and wheel pattern, coil/ignition arrangement, idle valve type, MAP/TPS and temperature sensors, pump relay/wiring, alternator and power distribution.

## Notes

Initial hardware may offer eight channels even when the chosen strategy uses four injector groups. Sequential operation requires appropriate phase recognition. A generic tune is not safe for first start.
